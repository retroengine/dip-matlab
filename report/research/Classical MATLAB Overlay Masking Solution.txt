Localisation Prior and Background Estimation
Morphological top-hat operations frequently generate false positives along high-contrast scene boundaries because natural edges share the localized high-frequency spatial characteristics of graphic overlays1. To overcome this limitation, background estimation must employ edge-preserving smoothing filters that decouple thin foreground alpha-blended objects from scene geometry2. The guided image filter provides an optimal framework for this task by assuming a local linear relationship between the guidance image and the filtering output, which effectively smooths out low-contrast, thin structural elements while retaining major object boundaries2.
